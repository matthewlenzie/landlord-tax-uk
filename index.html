Section 24 & HMOs

Mortgage interest relief restriction hits all landlords. But HMOs' higher yields help offset the impact - more income means Section 24 bites less proportionally.

Higher-yielding HMO investments can improve your post-tax returns.

SPV & Incorporation

Buying through a limited company (SPV) avoids Section 24. Corporation tax at 25% vs up to 45% income tax. But CGT on transfer and different finance products.

HMO Fund offers both personal and SPV products from their lender panel.

Allowable Expenses

Licensing fees, management costs, repairs, insurance, accountancy - all allowable. HMOs have more deductible costs than standard BTL.

Finance arrangement fees are often allowable. Ask your broker about fee structures.

CGT Planning

Capital gains on sale, principal private residence relief, annual exemptions, and transfer strategies. Plan exits carefully.

Rather than sell, consider refinancing to release equity - no CGT on borrowing.
